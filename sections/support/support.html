Deleting accounts

To delete your account, visit your profile on the app, and click "Delete Account." Confirm your choice, and all your data will be permanently removed.

We're here to help if you need assistance. Thank you for being a part of our community.

SMS

To unsubscribe from SMS messages reply "stop" to the sms thread.

To resubscribe to SMS messages reply "start" to the sms thread.

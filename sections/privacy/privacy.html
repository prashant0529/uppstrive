UppStrive Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

At UppStrive ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website, utilize our digital marketing and IT services, or interact with us.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when expressing an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and services, submitting inquiries through lead forms, or communicating with us directly.

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and project details provided in contact forms.
  • Usage & Device Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages viewed, referring URLs, and access times collected via web analytics.
  • Cookies & Tracking: Data collected through cookies, web beacons, and performance pixel tools to improve your browsing experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Delivering custom digital marketing, branding, AI solutions, and software development services.
  • Responding to inquiries, lead forms, and customer support requests.
  • Improving website performance, analytics, and user experience.
  • Sending relevant service updates, project proposals, and marketing communications.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers (such as web hosting, CRM tools, and analytics platforms) solely to perform services on our behalf, under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Data Retention & Security

We maintain technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Rights & Data Choices

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. To request account deletion or data removal, please visit your account profile or contact our team directly.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at info@uppstrive.com.
